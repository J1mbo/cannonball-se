-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Place the OutRun Revision B ROMs in this directory, either as loose files or as
a MAME ZIP archive.
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Instead of loose files, you can also drop a MAME ROM archive (e.g. outrun.zip,
merged or split) in here unextracted - no need to unzip it, and any subdirectories
or renamed files inside it are fine, since ROMs are matched by CRC32 and size,
not by filename. Loose files in this directory always take priority over an
archive. A same-named archive is also picked up next to the game executable or
in the current working directory, but only if it uses one of the MAME set names:
outrun.zip, outrunra.zip, outrundx.zip, outrundxa.zip, outrundxj.zip, outruneh.zip,
outruneha.zip, outrundxeh.zip, outrundxeha.zip, outrunb.zip (case-insensitive).

Fixed Audio Rom (Optional)
http://reassembler.blogspot.co.uk/2013/01/outrun-original-game-shipped-with.html
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

opr-10188.71f

With this option enabled in config.xml (sound.fix_samples), CannonBall-SE prefers,
in order: the official corrected ROM as named in MAME's "outruneh" set,
enhanced_203_opr-10188.71 (CRC c2de09b2); then this patched opr-10188.71f; then
falls back to the original opr-10188.71 if neither is present.
